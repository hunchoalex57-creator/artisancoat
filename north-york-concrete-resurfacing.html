Concrete Resurfacing in North York — FAQ

Straight answers to what North York homeowners ask us most.

How much does concrete resurfacing cost in North York?

Resurfacing typically costs 40–60% less than tearing out and replacing concrete. The exact price depends on the size and condition of your surface — a front porch and steps costs far less than a full pool deck or driveway. Every North York estimate is free and itemized, with no obligation. For fast ballpark pricing, send photos of your surface to us on WhatsApp.

Which North York neighbourhoods do you serve?

All of them — including Willowdale, Don Mills, Bayview Village, Lawrence Park North, York Mills, Newtonbrook, Bathurst Manor, Hoggs Hollow, and Banbury. If you're anywhere in North York or the surrounding Toronto area, we'll come to you for a free on-site estimate.

Will resurfacing hold up to North York winters and road salt?

Yes. We use frost-resistant, polymer-modified coatings engineered specifically for the GTA's freeze-thaw cycles and heavy winter salt use. Proper surface prep and sealing are what make the finish last for years — and that's the step we never skip.

Can you resurface my cracked North York porch instead of replacing it?

In most cases, yes. We diagnose the surface, repair the cracks and any structural damage first, then apply a fresh decorative finish over top — no demolition. Full replacement is only necessary when the underlying slab has failed structurally, which is rare.

How long does a typical North York project take?

Most porch, step, and patio projects are completed in 1–2 days. Larger pool decks or driveways may take a little longer. We'll give you an exact timeline with your free estimate, and we always leave the site clean.

How do I get a quote in North York without a site visit?
