What Affects the Final Price?

Five things move the needle. Anyone giving you a fixed price without seeing the surface is guessing.

1. Square Footage

Bigger jobs cost less per square foot. Setup time (prep, masking, mixing) is roughly the same for a small porch as a large patio. A 100 sq ft porch may run $20+/sq ft; an 800 sq ft driveway may run $10/sq ft.

2. Condition of the Existing Concrete

If the slab is sound with hairline cracks, prep is minimal. If there's spalling, deep cracks, exposed rebar, or settled sections, we have to repair before any decorative overlay can bond. Heavy prep can add $1,000–$3,000 to a project.

3. Finish Chosen

A simple stained and sealed concrete finish is the budget option. A Jewelstone stamped flagstone or ashlar slate pattern is mid-range. A custom multi-tone microcement floor is the premium tier. The price difference between low and high-end finishes on the same surface can be 2×.

4. Accessibility

A driveway we can park our truck next to is straightforward. A second-floor balcony, a backyard reached only through a narrow gate, or a basement that requires hauling materials down stairs adds labour cost.

5. Sealer & Warranty Tier

Our standard 3-year warranty includes a high-grade UV and freeze-thaw resistant sealer. Premium sealer upgrades (e.g., commercial-grade urethane topcoats) add cost but extend life by years.

Resurfacing vs. Replacement: Real Numbers

❌ Full Concrete Replacement

  • Driveway: $12,000 – $25,000
  • Pool Deck: $18,000 – $35,000
  • Porch: $5,000 – $12,000
  • Demolition + disposal fees
  • 1–2 weeks of work + curing
  • Lawn, irrigation, landscaping damage
  • New concrete cracks again in 5–10 yr

✓ Concrete Resurfacing

  • Driveway: $5,000 – $10,000
  • Pool Deck: $6,000 – $14,000
  • Porch: $2,000 – $4,000
  • No demolition
  • 2–3 days on most projects
  • Zero damage to surroundings
  • Flexible polymer overlay resists cracks
Bottom line: If your slab is structurally sound (no major settling or drainage problems), resurfacing gives you a better-looking, longer-lasting finish for roughly half the price of replacement.

Are your prices negotiable?

Our quotes are already at fair market rates — no inflated "sticker price" to discount from. We can adjust scope (finish tier, sealer grade, extras) to fit your budget, but we don't cut corners on prep.

Why do some contractors quote half your price?

Three reasons usually: (1) they're skipping proper crack repair and surface prep — the finish fails in 1–2 winters, (2) they're using lower-grade overlay materials not engineered for GTA freeze-thaw, (3) they're new and pricing for volume. We've been brought in to fix dozens of those jobs. Doing it right the first time costs more upfront but saves money long-term.

We don't offer in-house financing, but many GTA homeowners use a home equity line of credit or a credit card for projects over $5,000. We can supply detailed invoices for any financing application.
